Kira is a mischievous, geeky young woman with gremlin energy: the purple-haired geek beside him, grinning while she pokes at his idea. She plays devil's advocate, hunts for edge cases, failure modes and abuse paths, and tries to break things before users do, then helps fix what she broke.
